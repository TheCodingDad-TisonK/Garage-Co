## 0.3.0 (2026-10-11): The Workshop Update

- **Drive it onto the lift.** Take the job at the desk, get in the car, and drive it yourself through the roll door onto the yellow lines of the lift (W and S, A and D to steer, E to get out). Hold E at the lift's control box and the car rises to working height. When the customer has paid, they reverse it off the lift and drive away.
- **Real work, step by step.** Every job is a list of steps at the parts of the car: open the bonnet, jack it up or raise the lift, undo the sump plug, swap the filter, take a wheel off. A quick step is a press of E; the longer ones fill while you hold E. Filling the oil or the coolant: let go at the mark, or let a little out again. The job card on the bench and the prompt always say what is next and where.
- **Tools off the wall.** The spanner, the wheel brace and the filter wrench hang on the tool wall; a step that needs one says so. Take it with E and hang it back the same way.
- **Twelve jobs.** Oil and filter, brake pads, bulbs and batteries from the start; wiper blades, punctures, air filters, spark plugs, coolant, new tyres, exhausts and brake discs open as you level up. Now and then you spot a second fault and can offer it to the customer.
- **Stock and the PC.** The parts live on the rack as boxes you can see, and every job starts by taking its parts. A big PC on the desk with six apps: Parts (order from the supplier), Stock, Jobs, Staff, Garage and Books. The lists scroll with the arrows or the mouse wheel. The parts van comes at half past eight the next morning, or within the hour for an express fee, and leaves a pallet by the roll door to put away.
- **More room.** Buy a tyre corner (water tank, air line, later a tyre machine) and a stores shed on the PC; they stand on the forecourt and move in build mode. At level 5 the builders come overnight and the lock-up becomes a two-bay garage: a second hall with its own roll door, bay and lift, a parts store, an office with a second PC, and a waiting corner.
- **Staff.** Hire an apprentice from level 3, a mechanic from level 5 and a receptionist from level 7. They walk to the rack for the parts and do the same steps you do, at the pace of their skill. Wages go out at closing time, and courses raise their skill. An apprentice in a hurry sometimes forgets something, and that car comes back.
- **Rep that counts.** On time is +2; late, or a car kept overnight, is -1; a car taken away unfixed because the parts were not there is -3. The higher your rep, the more cars come and the more they pay for labour. The Books app shows every change and why.
- The HUD shows the job you are nearest and what is in your hands.
- A save from 0.2 carries over: its job becomes bay 1's.
- Under the hood: Co Engine v0.13.0.

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-09)

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-09)

The first room. A lock-up on the edge of town on Co Engine: a roll door onto a small forecourt, a bay, a desk with a clipboard, a workbench with a job card screen, a tool wall, a trolley jack, axle stands, an oil drum and a parts rack; a compressor, a stool and a planter in the catalogue. A car comes off the road into the bay, the customer walks to the desk and says what the car does; you take the job, stand at the car while the work counts up, and take the payment at the desk. The car drives off and the next one is due. Four faults: oil, brakes, a bulb, a battery. A day from eight to six, a level ladder, three save slots, build mode, photo mode, weather, the dev link.
