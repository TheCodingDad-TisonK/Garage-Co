## 0.2.0 (2026-10-09)

- **The roll door panel.** A screen on the wall beside the roll door, inside the lock-up: open and close the door from it, see whether a car is coming, how the job stands and what it pays.
- **The HUD.** The day, the time, the bank, rep, level, the job and the roll door along the top of the screen; the start screen shows the day, the bank and the jobs done.
- **An aiming dot** in the middle of the view, so a screen's button can be aimed at.
- **The customers' cars** have whole wheels that turn, arches along their sides, and windscreens that lean the right way.
- The faults a car comes in with are a tuning table the Co Engine editor's Settings tab can change.
- Under the hood: Co Engine v0.5.2.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-09)

The first room. A lock-up on the edge of town on Co Engine: a roll door onto a small forecourt, a bay, a desk with a clipboard, a workbench with a job card screen, a tool wall, a trolley jack, axle stands, an oil drum and a parts rack; a compressor, a stool and a planter in the catalogue. A car comes off the road into the bay, the customer walks to the desk and says what the car does; you take the job, stand at the car while the work counts up, and take the payment at the desk. The car drives off and the next one is due. Four faults: oil, brakes, a bulb, a battery. A day from eight to six, a level ladder, three save slots, build mode, photo mode, weather, the dev link.
